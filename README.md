# 📖 Overview

Human beings are inherently social creatures. From birth, humans depend on others for survival, learning, emotional development, and civilization itself.

This repository provides an educational module that examines human social nature through multiple scientific disciplines while integrating the Islamic worldview regarding human fitrah (natural disposition), family, and society.

The module aims to encourage critical thinking by connecting empirical scientific observations with ethical and spiritual perspectives.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Understand why humans are social beings.
- Explore biological and anthropological evidence of human social dependency.
- Learn the importance of family in human development.
- Examine the psychological impact of social relationships.
- Understand contemporary social challenges.
- Explore the Islamic perspective on human nature (fitrah).
- Encourage interdisciplinary learning between science and faith.

# 🧩 Learning Outcomes

After completing this module, learners will be able to:

- Explain why humans are considered social beings.
- Describe biological evidence supporting social cooperation.
- Understand the importance of family institutions.
- Analyze the effects of loneliness and social isolation.
- Discuss demographic challenges in modern societies.
- Explain the Islamic understanding of human social nature.
- Integrate scientific and ethical perspectives responsibly.
